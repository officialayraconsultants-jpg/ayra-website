Who We Are

Pakistan's Most Trusted Consultancy

AYRA Consultants was founded in 2014 with a single vision — to deliver world-class consulting solutions that drive measurable results for every client. Starting as a real estate consultancy in Karachi's DHA, we have grown into a full-service 360° business solutions firm operating pan-Pakistan and serving overseas clients globally.

Our agency is inspired by the greatest problem solvers and innovative thinkers. We believe truly great problem solving requires the re-imagination of what's possible — the ingenuity to see connections invisible to others — and the perfect balance between intuition and logic.

Today we proudly serve Pakistan's largest corporates including Levi's, Bahria Town, Civil Aviation Authority, Nishat Textiles, Junaid Jamshed and many more — with the same standard of excellence, integrity, and measurable results since day one.

Service 01

Lease & Retail
Management

Overview

Precision Lease Management

Geo-location serves as the prime aspect for any shopping hub or commercial space. At AYRA Consultants, we design a comprehensive feasibility report based on demographics and consumer behaviour — then allocate your premises with all possible benefits in mutual consent from developers and tenant.

What We Offer

  • Geo-location feasibility reports and demographic surveys
  • Site identification, allocation and negotiation
  • Lease documentation — MOUs, agreements, terminations
  • Renewal and renegotiation management
  • Retainerships and long-term stakeholder management
  • Legal compliance with highly skilled in-house lawyers
  • Performance indicators — foot traffic, capture rate, conversion
  • Ethical stakeholder and vendor management

Our Approach

We administer lease management as per our feasibility report and demographic survey — working with our team of highly skilled lawyers to ensure stakeholders and commercial vendors are honoured with ethical aspects.
