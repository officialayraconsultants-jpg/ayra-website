Service 01

Lease & Retail
Management

Overview

Precision Lease Management

Geo-location serves as the prime aspect for any shopping hub or commercial space. At AYRA Consultants, we design a comprehensive feasibility report based on demographics and consumer behaviour — then allocate your premises with all possible benefits in mutual consent from developers and tenant.

What We Offer

  • Geo-location feasibility reports and demographic surveys
  • Site identification, allocation and negotiation
  • Lease documentation — MOUs, agreements, terminations
  • Renewal and renegotiation management
  • Retainerships and long-term stakeholder management
  • Legal compliance with highly skilled in-house lawyers
  • Performance indicators — foot traffic, capture rate, conversion
  • Ethical stakeholder and vendor management

Our Approach

We administer lease management as per our feasibility report and demographic survey — working with our team of highly skilled lawyers to ensure stakeholders and commercial vendors are honoured with ethical aspects. We maintain a fine route to renewals, renegotiations, MOUs, terminations and retainerships with full transparency and decency.

Market Coverage

Our lease management services cover all major commercial hubs across Pakistan — Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Quetta — with active expansion into tier-2 cities.
